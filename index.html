Skillego

Hidden-information strategy · Capture all enemy pieces to win

How to Play

All 18 pieces per side start face-down on a 6×6 board. On your turn, either flip a covered piece to reveal it, or move an uncovered piece one step (orthogonal).

Piece hierarchy (higher captures lower):

🐉Dragon×1
🤖Robot×1
🧙‍♂️Wizard×2
🐶Dog×4
😸Cat×4
🐭Mouse×6

Special rule: 🐭 Mouse captures 🐉 Dragon — but Dragon beats everything else.

Eliminate all opponent pieces to win.
